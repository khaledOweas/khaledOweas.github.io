About

An engineer, not a checklist of frameworks

I'm a Senior/Lead-level software engineer with more than ten years building, maintaining, modernizing, and optimizing software across enterprise and product environments. My strongest foundation is the .NET ecosystem — C#, ASP.NET Core, Web API, SQL Server, Entity Framework — complemented by deep frontend experience in Angular and, more recently, React with TypeScript.

Based in Egypt, I work remote-only with US companies across American timezones — currently at Infinite Software Solutions, Inc. My career spans healthcare, banking, insurance, government, manufacturing, and international software companies. More recently that's expanded into multi-tenant and multi-database architecture, application performance investigation, cloud troubleshooting, CI/CD pipelines with custom build agents, and dynamic reporting platforms.

Outside of full-time roles, I've independently designed and evolved production healthcare ecosystems — web, backend, database, and role-specific mobile apps live on the App Store and Google Play — combining WhatsApp Cloud API, payment gateways, real-time notifications, and operational dashboards used daily by patients, doctors, and staff.

I'm not looking to be known for a long list of technologies. I take responsibility for whether a complex system actually works — in architecture, integration, performance, and production.
